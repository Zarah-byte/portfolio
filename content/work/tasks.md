## Problem

To-do apps became anxiety machines. Every unchecked box is a small accusation, and
the more features an app added — projects, tags, priorities, calendars — the more
the list itself became the work. People were managing their task manager instead
of their tasks.

## Approach

One thing, then the next. Tasks answers a single question and hides the rest: the
default view is the one thing to do now. Everything else — the backlog, the
someday, the maybe — is deliberately one deliberate tap away, so the list can't
guilt you from the home screen.

## Outcome

Fewer lists, more finished. Constraining the interface to the present moment
turned an anxiety surface into a focus tool — the backlog still existed, it just
stopped being the first thing you saw.
