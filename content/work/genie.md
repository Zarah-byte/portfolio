## Problem

Capture is easy; follow-through isn't. Everyone has a graveyard of notes apps,
starred messages, and voice memos. The idea gets saved and then never seen again.
The gap isn't storage — it's the quiet, unglamorous step of turning a note into a
task with a next move.

## Approach

Suggest, don't dictate. Genie proposes structure — a task, a due date, a grouping —
and lets you accept it with a glance. The AI stays a collaborator, never a boss:
every suggestion is one tap to keep, one tap to dismiss, and the interface makes
the *decline* as easy as the accept so trust builds instead of eroding.

## Outcome

From backlog to done. By surfacing the next action instead of the archive, Genie
turned passive capture into momentum — the notes finally led somewhere.
