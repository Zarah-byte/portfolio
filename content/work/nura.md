## Problem

Care between appointments falls through the cracks. The hardest part of a chronic
condition isn't the clinic visit — it's the ninety days in between, where tracking
is tedious, easy to skip, and quietly guilt-inducing. Existing tools treated people
like data-entry clerks for their own bodies.

## Approach

Soft edges, honest data. Nura asks for less, more kindly: one tap to log how you
feel, a routine that adapts to the day instead of nagging, and a tone that never
scolds a missed dose. Underneath the calm surface, the data is rigorous — clean
enough to bring to a doctor, gentle enough to keep up with.

## Outcome

Consistency without pressure. In testing, participants logged more often precisely
because the app asked for less, and described it as a companion rather than a
tracker — the exact shift the thesis set out to prove.
