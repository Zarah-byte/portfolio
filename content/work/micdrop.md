## Problem

Voice is intimate, but the tools are clumsy. By the time you've found the record
button, watched a waveform, trimmed the ends, and picked a recipient, the impulse
that made you want to speak is gone. The friction quietly kills the most human
format we have.

## Approach

One gesture, no menus. Press to record, release to send — the whole interaction
collapses into a single motion. Everything optional (trimming, captions, replies)
lives *after* the send, never in the way of it. The design protects spontaneity as
the product's most valuable, most fragile feature.

## Outcome

Spontaneity, restored. Removing the menus removed the hesitation — people shared
more, and more honestly, because the tool got out of the way of the moment.
