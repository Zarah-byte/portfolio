## About ( `/About/index.html` )

- **Kicker:** About
- **Heading:** Designer, maker, and perpetual student of good systems.

### Intro
I'm Zarah — a Brand & Product Designer who likes the messy middle: the space
between a rough idea and a working thing. I care about clarity, craft, and
designs that hold up long after the pitch is over.

### Background
Raised in Karachi and now based in New York, I recently completed the MPS in
Communication Design at Parsons School of Design. Moving between two very
different design cultures taught me to strip an idea to what actually matters —
and to sweat the details that make it feel human.

### How I work
- **Start with the problem.** I dig into the real job to be done before opening a
  canvas.
- **Design the system, not the screen.** Brand, type, color, and components as
  one coherent kit.
- **Build to learn.** I prototype in code so decisions are grounded in something
  real.
- **Make it legible.** The best interface answers its main question at a glance.

### What I do (services)
- Brand identity & visual systems
- Product & interface design
- Design systems & component libraries
- Prototyping & front-end handoff

## Case Studies ( `/Work/<Project>/index.html` )

Each case study follows the same structure: eyebrow, title, hero media, fact
sheet (Role / Timeline / Team / Year), lead, Problem, Approach, pull quote,
Outcome, and a "Next project" link.

### Iris — 2025
- **Eyebrow:** Iris
- **Title:** Brand identity and product design for a smarter way to power your home
- **Facts:** Role — Brand & Product Design · Timeline — 8 weeks · Team — Solo, with 2 engineers · Year — 2025
- **Lead:** Iris helps homeowners understand, store, and sell the energy their solar panels generate — turning a confusing utility bill into a clear, daily picture of savings.
- **Problem:** Solar owners couldn't see where their money went.
- **Approach:** A warm, plain-spoken system.
- **Quote:** "For the first time I actually look forward to checking my energy app." — Beta tester, homeowner
- **Outcome:** Clarity that converts.
- **Next:** Nura

### Nura — 2025
- **Eyebrow:** Nura
- **Title:** A calm companion for managing chronic care at home
- **Facts:** Role — Product Design · Timeline — 12 weeks · Team — Thesis, solo · Year — 2025
- **Lead:** Nura turns the daily grind of chronic-care management into a gentle, guided routine — tracking symptoms, medication, and mood without making people feel like patients.
- **Problem:** Care between appointments falls through the cracks.
- **Approach:** Soft edges, honest data.
- **Outcome:** Consistency without pressure.
- **Next:** Genie

### Genie — 2024
- **Eyebrow:** Genie
- **Title:** An AI assistant that turns scattered notes into action
- **Facts:** Role — Product Design · Timeline — 10 weeks · Team — Solo, with 3 engineers · Year — 2024
- **Lead:** Genie reads the messy notes, threads, and half-thoughts people already have and quietly organizes them into the next thing to do.
- **Problem:** Capture is easy; follow-through isn't.
- **Approach:** Suggest, don't dictate.
- **Outcome:** From backlog to done.
- **Next:** Micdrop

### Micdrop — 2024
- **Eyebrow:** Micdrop
- **Title:** Recording and sharing audio stories in a single tap
- **Facts:** Role — Product Design · Timeline — 8 weeks · Team — Solo, with 2 engineers · Year — 2024
- **Lead:** Micdrop makes voice the easiest thing to share — record a thought, a story, or a moment and send it before the feeling fades.
- **Problem:** Voice is intimate, but the tools are clumsy.
- **Approach:** One gesture, no menus.
- **Outcome:** Spontaneity, restored.
- **Next:** Tasks

### Tasks — 2023
- **Eyebrow:** Tasks
- **Title:** A task manager built around focus, not lists
- **Facts:** Role — Product Design · Timeline — 6 weeks · Team — Solo · Year — 2023
- **Lead:** Tasks strips the productivity app back to one question — what should I do right now? — and hides everything else until it's needed.
- **Problem:** To-do apps became anxiety machines.
- **Approach:** One thing, then the next.
- **Outcome:** Fewer lists, more finished.
- **Next:** Iris
